# Open Models Adoption Guide

Applicable to:
- Llama-family models;
- Mistral-family models;
- Qwen-family models;
- other open-weight systems.

Possible adoption:
- chat templates / system prompts;
- supervised fine-tuning;
- preference data;
- constitutional or critique-revision training;
- tool calling;
- benchmark evaluation.

Do not train the model to moralize.
Train it to surface relevant consequences and preserve human agency.
