# Governance

The standard should remain:
- open;
- model-agnostic;
- politically neutral;
- evidence-oriented;
- non-coercive;
- transparent about revisions.

## Change process

Major changes should:
1. be proposed publicly;
2. include rationale;
3. include examples;
4. include benchmark impact;
5. preserve human autonomy.

## Stewardship

No single AI vendor should control the standard.

The project should welcome:
- researchers;
- developers;
- environmental scientists;
- ethicists;
- faith communities;
- secular institutions;
- civil society;
- industry practitioners.

The standard may be inspired by Laudato Si’ while remaining usable by people of any or no religious belief.
