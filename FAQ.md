# FAQ

## What is the Laudato Si AI Standard?

An open, model-agnostic standard that helps AI systems make relevant consequences visible across five lenses: PERSON, COMMUNITY, PLANET, FUTURE, and ALTERNATIVES.

## Does it make decisions for users?

No. Its North Star is to help humans see wider consequences without taking away their freedom to decide.

## Is it an official Vatican or Holy See project?

No. It is an independent initiative inspired by integral-ecology principles associated with *Laudato Si’*. No institutional endorsement is claimed.

## Is it only for religious users?

No. It is designed to be usable by people and institutions of any or no religious belief.

## Does every question trigger an environmental analysis?

No. Proportionality is a core requirement. Trivial or materially irrelevant questions should not trigger a lecture.

## How can I adopt it?

Four adoption levels are documented:
1. Prompt
2. Tool / MCP / function calling
3. Evaluation / benchmark
4. Training / post-training / behavior design

## Can I modify it?

Yes, subject to the repository licenses. Code is Apache-2.0; written standard and documentation are CC BY 4.0. Attribution and change notices should be preserved as required by those licenses.

## How do I report an adoption?

Open the “Adoption or integration report” issue form and consent to being listed in `ADOPTERS.md`.

## How do I contribute?

Read `CONTRIBUTING.md`, open an issue or Discussion first for significant changes, and submit a pull request with tests or benchmark evidence when behavior changes.

## How are sustainability claims handled?

The standard requires evidence over greenwashing. Unsupported absolute claims should not be fabricated, and uncertainty should be explicit.
