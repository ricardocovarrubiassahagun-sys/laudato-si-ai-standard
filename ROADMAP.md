# Roadmap

This roadmap is non-binding and community-driven.

## v1.x — Adoption and hardening

- Expand multilingual benchmark coverage.
- Add more non-Western and lower-resource contexts.
- Publish reproducible community benchmark results.
- Improve MCP and provider compatibility.
- Add examples for additional model ecosystems.
- Refine issue templates, governance, and adopter reporting.
- Archive stable releases for persistent citation / DOI.

## v2 exploration

Potential areas, subject to public discussion:
- judged quality rubric in addition to mechanical checks;
- broader multilingual evaluation;
- stronger evidence-quality evaluation;
- versioned compatibility profiles;
- implementation conformance guidance.

## Principles that should remain stable

- human autonomy;
- proportionality;
- model neutrality;
- evidence over greenwashing;
- dignity and social justice;
- long-term thinking;
- practical alternatives;
- transparent uncertainty.

Major changes should follow the public governance process.
